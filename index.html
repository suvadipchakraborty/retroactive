Retroactive
Tech history, one day at a time.

Retroactive pulls the day's tech-relevant record straight from Wikipedia's "on this day" archive — the computers that shipped, the sites that launched, the patents filed, the people born and lost. Step back a day, jump ahead, or shuffle to a random date entirely. File a story to keep it in your own record, and flip the switch in the top corner if you'd rather read it off a green phosphor screen.
